These files are original upstream WOFF2 binaries, renamed only for URL
consistency. No glyphs, metadata, outlines, hinting, or font names were modified.
Regular and italic are weight 400; semibold and semibold italic are weight 600.
The regular fonts contain 920 Unicode mappings; italics contain 919.
Latin, accented Latin, smart quotes, en/em dashes, ellipsis, and every character
in the repository's existing article bodies were verified against the cmap.
